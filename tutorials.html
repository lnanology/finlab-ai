Feature Tutorials

How to use every free tool on XFINLAB — real screenshots, plain steps, and what's worth knowing before you trust the number on screen.

1. AI Chart Analysis

Type any global ticker and get real technical analysis — trend, support/resistance, and a confidence-scored signal computed from real price history, not an AI guess.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Chart Analysis in the top nav, or xfinlab.com/chart-analysis.html
  2. Type a ticker — US stocks (AAPL), Hong Kong (0700 or 0700.HK), China A-shares (600519), or crypto (BTC)
  3. Tap Analyze
  4. Read the trend, support/resistance levels, and confluence signal
The confidence % reflects how many underlying technical signals agree with each other — it is not a price prediction.

2. Black Swan Stress Test Lab

See how a strategy or ticker would have survived real historical crises (2008, 2020 COVID crash) using an actual Monte Carlo simulation over real historical returns.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Stress Lab
  2. Pick a strategy preset (e.g. Stocks/Bonds 60/40) or a ticker for the Monte Carlo section
  3. Enter your investment amount
  4. Tap Run Simulation and review the outcome ranges and max drawdown
The page states plainly this is "estimates based on historical event declines, not a full historical price backtest" — worth reading before you act on it.

3. Stock Screener

Pick a preset or set your own criteria and get an AI-written screening report explaining which stocks fit and why.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Screener
  2. Tap a quick preset (High Growth, Value Investing, High Dividend...) or set Market/Sector/Market Cap/Growth/Valuation/Risk manually
  3. Tap AI Smart Screening and read the written analysis
This returns written analysis, not a sortable table of tickers with numeric scores — a research starting point, not a ranked list to trade off directly.

4. Multi-Company Benchmark Analysis

Compare real-time market data for up to 5 companies at once, with AI-generated commentary on strengths, weaknesses, risks and investment angles.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Compare
  2. Enter up to 5 tickers (Company 1–5)
  3. Tap Start Benchmark Analysis

5. Anomaly Detection Radar

Scans your watchlist (or a default basket if not logged in) using real volume and price data to surface unusual moves — every metric comes from real market data, not AI guesswork.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Anomaly Detection
  2. Search a single ticker to check it on its own, or tap Start Scan to scan your whole watchlist
  3. Review the overall severity and any flagged tickers

6. Portfolio Allocation Analysis

Uses each of your watchlist stocks' real market scores (or a default basket if not logged in) to suggest allocation weights, so you can see how diversified your portfolio really is.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Portfolio
  2. Tap Calculate Allocation — uses your real watchlist if logged in, otherwise a default basket
  3. Review the suggested weight per ticker
Weights come from each ticker's real market score, not equal-weighted and not something you set manually — a comparison point, not an auto-rebalancing tool.

7. News Denoise Analysis

Enter a stock ticker or market topic and AI organizes the latest news highlights, filtering out sensationalized hype language, into a concise summary.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to News Denoise
  2. Type a ticker, topic, or question (e.g. AAPL, "Fed rate decision")
  3. Tap Start Analysis
Generated live by AI per-request, not pulled from a structured news database — wording will vary between calls even for the same topic, by design.

8. Probability Scan

Combines real market data, technical indicators, and news sentiment into a bullish/bearish probability read — not trading advice.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Probability Scan
  2. Enter a ticker
  3. Tap Scan
The page's own disclaimer: the internal scoring formulas haven't been calibrated with historical backtesting yet — a reference number, not a calibrated probability.

9. Pairs Statistical Arbitrage Scanner

Monitors the real historical price relationship between two tickers and flags when today's spread has moved unusually far from its own historical norm.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to Pairs Trading
  2. Enter Symbol A and Symbol B (e.g. KO and PEP)
  3. Pick a lookback period (default 6mo)
  4. Tap Scan Pair and review the z-score, correlation, and richer/cheaper read
This is "a correlation + z-score divergence scan — not a formal cointegration test," per the page itself.

10. AI Quick Market Analysis

The fastest single entry point: enter a stock/ETF/crypto symbol and get multi-dimensional scores and a probability reference from real market data and news, in one place.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to AI Analysis
  2. Search any global asset and tap Run Analysis
  3. Expand the sections you care about: AI Reasoning, Detailed Scoring, Probability Analysis, Support & Resistance, Risk Factors, AI Conclusion
Logged-in users also get a full Stock Analysis dashboard with Final Score / Price / Risk Level / AI Rating, a personal watchlist, and their Intelligence API key in one screen.

11. AI Chat

Institutional-grade AI financial analysis in a conversational form — enter a ticker, company name, or upload a chart image, and get an in-depth report instantly.

Desktop
Mobile
  1. Go to AI Chat
  2. Type a ticker or company name, or tap the paperclip to upload a chart image
  3. Try a quick action — Market Fund Flow, Market Sentiment Analysis, or Trending Assets — or just ask your own question
Analysis is for reference only and does not constitute investment advice — the same disclaimer appears directly under the chat input.
